- Breaks, new: after 20 minutes at the Mac (or 30, 45 or 60), a short break to look into the distance, announced on the notch ten seconds before. Every screen is blurred with a card that counts down and lets you skip the break or have it in five minutes; it waits through meetings and full-screen apps, and can remind you to blink and to sit up.
- Browser, new: the Figghost Companion extension for Chrome, Edge, Brave, Vivaldi and Arc blocks the sites you list while a Pomodoro work session runs, and sends a page or a link to Video download. It reads no page, and Remove all on its page takes away everything it put outside the app.
- System: the processor load, the memory in use and the network speed can stay in the menu bar; a click on them opens the System tab.
- Screen Time: count by site too, for the tab in front of Safari or a Chromium browser. Only the name of the site is kept, and switching it off forgets them.
- Utilities: switch the dark appearance, hide the desktop icons for a clean screen to share (they come back when the utilities are switched off), and copy the link of the browser tab in front with Control-Option-Command-L, as Markdown, the address alone, or the title and the address.
