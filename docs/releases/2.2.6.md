- Settings window: on macOS 26 the sidebar and the buttons of the toolbar are the system's own Liquid Glass, the sidebar showing what is behind the window, and the search sits at the right of the toolbar, as in Finder.
- System in the menu bar: the load of the graphics processor, the heat of the processor and the room on the startup disk can stay there too, beside the processor, the memory and the network. Each shows as text, as a small label over its value, as a graph of the last thirty readings, or for the processor as a bar for each core; all in one item or each in its own, in the colour of the menu bar or with the graphs in the accent colour. Every figure keeps its width as it changes.
- Launcher: pins come in up to eight groups, and a pin can be a file or a website as well as an app, a folder or a Shortcut. The tab moves between the groups with its strip, a swipe of two fingers, the wheel or the arrow keys; a right click on a folder lists what is in it, and on an app or a file offers Open and Show in Finder.
- Launcher dock: with its switch on, the groups also sit on Liquid Glass at the left, right or bottom edge of a screen, above the windows, and can stay hidden until the pointer comes. It is off by default.
- Scrolling: the wheel of another app, such as a remote desktop host, is left alone, so a Chrome Remote Desktop session no longer freezes for a moment after each scroll.
- Windows beta: the zip carries the WebView2Loader.dll the program needs to start.
