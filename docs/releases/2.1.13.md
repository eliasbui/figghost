- Weather: a moving sky now plays behind the tab. The sun or the moon sits where it is in the day, and there are clouds, drizzle or heavy rain with ripples, lightning in thunderstorms, snow, frost when it is cold, wind streaks with leaves that swirl in a gale (in the colour of the season), fog and the dust of dirty air. It stays still when Reduce Motion is on.
- Weather: a new Details view (the button at the right of the line over the cards) tells the season, the rain (size of the drops, thunder, UV), the wind (strength, direction, gusts) and how it feels, with the air quality index, fine dust against the WHO limit and what the air suits: exercise, open windows, a mask, children and the elderly.
- Weather: optional soft rain, wind and thunder sounds that match the sky, off by default, in Settings under Weather.
