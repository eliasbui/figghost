- Jarvis (beta, off by default, under Typing and text): an assistant in a chat on Liquid Glass. A shortcut (Control+Option+Space) opens it, it asks what it can do, listens in English or Vietnamese, and writes the answer as it comes, reading each sentence aloud. A character at the top left rises when it opens and moves while Jarvis listens, thinks and speaks, with a soft sound for waking, answering and errors.
- Jarvis asks Anthropic, OpenAI, Gemini or DeepSeek with a key kept in the Keychain, a model on this Mac (Ollama), or the ChatGPT plan through the sign-in of Codex, which is only read. Turning it on sends what you type or say there to the service you choose.
