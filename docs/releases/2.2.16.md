- Network: History keeps what each app moved over 90 days, by connection, even while the page is closed, with today, 7 days, 30 days, a bar for each day and an export to CSV or JSON in Downloads. It is off until turned on.
- Network: Connections lists each connection with how well it answers, a speed test of it (the system's own test, about fifteen seconds) and, for Wi-Fi, the signal, noise, channel, band and speed of the link.
- Network: Save data keeps the apps you do not allow off a connection that is paid by the byte (a phone's, found by its name and address, or marked by hand), with a data cap. A wire to Block refuses an app, and a data quota for a day or a month refuses an app that has moved that much. The notch says so at 80 % and at 100 %. It applies to apps that follow the proxy settings.
- Network: the card of an app on the diagram lists the sites it talks to, by name for the apps that go through the proxy and by address for the others.
- Network: the notch can say when an app uploads far more than it usually does, when the internet is lost or back, and when a network such as a hotel's asks for a login. Make the profile of an encrypted DNS (Cloudflare, Quad9, AdGuard, Google, NextDNS or your own) and macOS asks you to approve it.
- Rewrite: select text anywhere and a glass window shows it fixed, more formal, shorter or in English, written by the model that Jarvis asks. Return copies it or pastes it over the selection.
- Command palette: one shortcut and a field to type in to open a tab or a page, turn a feature on or off, go to the agent that waits, test a connection or add a reminder in a few words ("call mom tomorrow 9h"). A few addresses (`figghost://palette`, `page`, `tab`, `agents/jump`, `remind`) do the same from a link or Shortcuts.
- Mirror: the camera, mirrored, under the notch, and a teleprompter that scrolls the last note you wrote near the lens, left out of screenshots and recordings.
- Commands: a tab of the notch with a few saved commands; press one and what it prints scrolls in the tab.
- AI agents: each session now knows its terminal, so the shortcut or address that goes to the agent that waits longest brings that terminal (Orca, or the terminal program) to the front.
- Mole: lists the servers, dev servers and test browsers that an agent left running after it ended, and stops the ones you choose.
- Notifications: an optional red count of the unread beside the closed notch.
- Browser: the extension can set the speed of the videos that play, after you allow it once from the menu of its button.
- Live islands: the notice of the camera names the app in front, as a guess.
