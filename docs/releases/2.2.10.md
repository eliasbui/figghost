- Network (off by default, under This Mac): which app uses the network, how fast and through which connection, in a list and in a diagram with Wi-Fi, Ethernet and a phone on USB on the left, Combined and Automatic in the middle and the apps on the right. Watching reads the system's own figures once a second while the page is open and needs no permission.
- Wiring (beta): drag an app to one connection or to Combined (every connection at once), and give it a speed limit and a priority; apps wired after the first one to a place give way to it when that connection is full. It applies to the apps that follow the proxy settings of macOS, which Figghost points at itself and puts back when it is turned off or the app quits. macOS may ask for an administrator password.
- Share with a phone (beta): an Android phone on USB takes the Mac's connection through gnirehtet, which you install yourself.
