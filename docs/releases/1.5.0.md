- Settings: a new look with a ghost that follows the pointer, a sky that changes with the time of day and handwritten titles, a "Notch tabs" group for the pages of the notch's tabs, Command-K to search, and a page for every new feature in English and Vietnamese.
- Notch tabs: nine new tabs that join the strip when you turn them on, so a notch that you have not changed keeps its seven tabs. System (processor, memory, network, disk, heat), Notes, Launcher (apps, folders and Apple Shortcuts), Weather, Pull requests (GitHub and GitLab), Pomodoro, Screen Time, Day progress and Devices.
- Devices: the charge of the Bluetooth devices that are connected, earbuds bud by bud, with a warning when one runs low and the last charge a device reported kept for two hours.
- Live islands in the closed notch: Caps Lock, the charger, a battery that is full or low, a drive that arrives or leaves with an Eject entry, a finished download, and the camera or microphone being used.
- Utilities: keep the Mac awake, mute the microphone for every app, lock the keyboard while you clean it, and snooze the notch so that it does not open when the pointer reaches the top of the screen.
- Safer: a setting of the wrong kind in the file no longer resets everything, notes that are too long to edit are left alone and never cut, the right drive is ejected, a name can no longer be made to read as another, and a command that hangs is stopped with what it started.
