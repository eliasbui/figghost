- Notifications, new on the notch: the notifications your apps send, read from Notification Center as they come. Choose how they arrive: through the system, with macOS's banners as before and a Notifications tab that keeps the list, newest first with each app's icon; or through the notch, where each one comes out of the closed notch at once with the app's icon, its title and its words.
- Notifications: on the notch a notification comes before everything else, the music, the date and time and every other live notice, and goes again after the seconds you choose; a click on a row of the tab opens the app that sent it, and what they say can be kept off the notch.
- Notifications: macOS keeps notifications where only an app with Full Disk Access may read them, so the page opens that list in System Settings, and the Notifications settings too, to turn off macOS's banners for the apps that should come through the notch. Figghost only reads them, only when something changes; nothing is written, kept or sent anywhere.
