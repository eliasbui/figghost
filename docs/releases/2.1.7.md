- Displays: every display gets a card on the Displays tab, the built-in one too, with a menu of its color profiles: its own and the ones macOS comes with, such as Display P3, sRGB, Adobe RGB, Rec. 709 and Rec. 2020, as System Settings lists them. The picture changes at once, and choosing the display's own profile puts it back. A switch in Settings, Displays, turns the menus off.
