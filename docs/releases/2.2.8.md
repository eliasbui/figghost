- Dictation (off by default, under Typing and text): with the caret in a text box, a shortcut (Control+Option+V) brings a microphone to the middle of the screen on Liquid Glass, with a meter that moves with your voice and the words as they are understood. Return or the shortcut again puts them in, Escape throws them away, and the arrows go on in the other language. English and Vietnamese are recognized on the Mac itself unless you allow Apple's servers, and a silence after the words puts them in.
- Settings window: the window's own colour now lies over its glass, so what is behind the window only shows faintly and the page is easy to read, in the light look and the dark one.
