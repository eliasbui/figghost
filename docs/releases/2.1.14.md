- Weather: the sky behind the tab is now drawn like a photograph, by the graphics chip. The sun and the moon cross the sky as the day goes, clouds are lit from the side of the sun and dark at their flat bases, and a sunset turns the horizon gold. Rain falls in three depths, fine in a drizzle and thick in a downpour, slanted by the wind; lightning lights the clouds up with a branching bolt, and snow comes down in flakes that are sharp far away and soft near. Frost creeps in from the edges when it is cold, dirty air browns the sky and reddens the sun, and in a gale leaves tumble and curl through it.
- Weather: nothing of this is started until a sky is first shown, so a Mac that never opens the Weather tab pays nothing for it, and the sky stands still when Reduce Motion is on.
- Settings: every page of the settings window is now in a glass sidebar, under five headings (Overview, Notch & Island, Notch tabs, Mouse & Windows, Clipboard & Tools), each page with an icon of its own and a green dot while its feature is on. A heading folds its group away, and the search is at the top of the sidebar (Command-K).
- Settings: every page opens on a hero in its own colours with the switch that turns its feature on and off, the General page has a tile for each feature (the circle turns it on and off, the name opens its page), and the toolbar has Back and Forward.
