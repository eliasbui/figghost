- Displays: with two or more displays connected, the tab has a second view, Arrange, with the displays drawn where they are, as in System Settings. Drag a display to move it and it goes flush against the nearest side of another; drag the white bar onto another display to make that one the main display; its menu chooses what a display is used as: the main display, an extended display, or a mirror of another.
- Present, a new tab of the notch: show a whole display, an area, one window or every window of an app to an audience, full screen on another display, a projector or a TV, or in a window of its own that a video call can share. The notch and every window of Figghost stay out of the picture, and a thin green frame on your screen marks what is shown.
- Present: a click zooms the audience's picture in where it lands and the view follows the pointer, until a quiet moment takes it back to the whole picture. A spotlight around the pointer, a ring around it, a ripple for each click, a held picture and a black or white screen are a key or a click away, and the time runs in the closed notch.
- Present: Control-Option-P starts and stops from any app. While presenting, Control-Option with Z and X zooms in and out, 0 shows the whole picture, A turns the automatic zoom on and off, L lights the spotlight, M rings the pointer, H holds the picture, and B and W blank the screen as in slide shows. Every key can be changed on the Present page, and only the one that starts is held while nothing is presented.
