- Notch: a notification on the closed notch is one row, the app's icon at the left and its title and words beside it in larger type. On a screen with a camera cutout the row sits below the camera; on a screen without one it starts at the top edge and is shorter. A notification without an app icon shows a blue tile with a bell.
- Settings: Notch, Camera cutout chooses where a notification goes: Automatic (as the screen is), Has a cutout, or No cutout.
- This version is for macOS only; there is no Windows or Linux beta of it.
