- Cursor: the arrow and the hand over links can become a rainbow arrow, a pixel character that blinks, or any picture or GIF of your own, with its own size and speed (off by default)
- Always on top: a shortcut, or a switch in the menu bar panel, keeps the window in front above all the others with a red border round it; while another window covers it a live picture of it shows over the cover, and a click on the picture brings the real window forward (off by default; needs Accessibility and Screen Recording)
- Shortcuts page: the keys inside the notch and the clipboard popup (pin, file in a board, paste the other way, delete) can be changed like any shortcut, and the page lists every shortcut of the app in one place
- Drag wheel: any key can open a wheel while files are dragged, not only Shift, Command, Control and Option
- Settings: a search field at the top of the sidebar (Command+F) finds a page by what its settings say
- Fixed: the Claude usage limits in the notch stopped updating after the app moved to another folder; the buttons of the What's new window sat off centre
- Area picker for screenshots and the colour picker, as simple as the one macOS draws: the system crosshair with the pixel position beside it, no magnifier, no lines across the screen and no hint bar; the screen darkens only while you drag
- The clipboard list hides its wide scroll bar; it still scrolls with the trackpad, the wheel and the arrow keys
