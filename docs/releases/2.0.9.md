- Snippets: type an abbreviation in any app, such as ;sig, and it becomes the text you saved for it: an email reply, an address, a piece of code. It works with Vietnamese typing on or off. Turn it on in Settings, under Clipboard and Tools.
- Video download: paste a link from YouTube, Facebook, Vimeo or another site and the video is saved to Downloads, with its progress on the closed notch. It uses yt-dlp, which you install yourself (brew install yt-dlp); without ffmpeg, Figghost joins the picture and the sound itself, up to 1080p on YouTube.
- Mole: the disk usage list has a ring chart above it; click a slice to open that folder.
- Drag wheel: a video can become a GIF next to the original.
