- A hello from the ghost: each time you open Figghost yourself, its ghost comes down out of the notch, blinks once and goes back up; never at login or in the background, and it can be turned off on the General page
- Translate: select text anywhere and press the shortcut, and a glass window next to the pointer translates it into the language you choose
- Bluetooth in the notch: when headphones, a speaker, a keyboard or a mouse connects or drops, the closed notch says so for a moment
- 3D sound for headphones in the Sound tab: the two channels become two speakers around your head, with their direction and a slow turn of your own choosing
- Snap in two steps: drag a window to the top edge, point at a layout to see its cells on the screen, then go down into a cell and release; the row shrinks to a chip while you choose
- Layouts of your own: divide the screen into a grid, join parts of it into cells, and choose which layouts each screen offers
- Notch size styles: pick Compact, Standard or Wide with a drawing of each, or set the open and closed width and height yourself
- The notch keeps one width from tab to tab, and on a Mac with a notch its header sits beside the camera, so the open notch has no empty band across its top
- Bigger tab icons in the notch, and fainter text that is easier to read; the key hints at the bottom of a tab show only while you use the keyboard there
- The notch waits a little longer under the pointer before it opens (300 ms), so it no longer opens when the pointer only passes by
- The Sound tab matches the rest of the notch: white sliders, a speaker to mute each app, and rounded menus
- The settings window and the menu bar panel take the accent colour you chose in System Settings, show what is on or granted in green, and use the system's own symbols
- Clearer controls in the settings window: larger buttons, bold section titles, checkboxes for the formats of the Drag wheel, and the three tasks of Mole on one card
- What's new after an update: a short tour that opens by itself the first time a new version runs, as slides when there is a lot
- Capture shortcuts: two new ones on the Capture page, to capture again with the same kind as the last time and to open the folder of the captures
- Updates keep your permissions: Accessibility stays granted after an update, so it no longer has to be allowed again each time (the update from 0.8.0 asks for it one last time)
- A smaller menu bar panel: simpler, with the new icon, and a right click on the icon no longer opens a second menu
- A tidier settings sidebar: it no longer sits against the red, yellow and green window buttons
- The About page tells what Figghost is, why it exists and how its licence lets you use it
- A dark outline around the open notch no longer stays behind after the notch changes size
- The Drag wheel icon shows in the sidebar, and the sidebar keeps its icons when the window is short
