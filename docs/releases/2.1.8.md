- Notch: at rest, the notch shows the date and the time with a previous and a next button. On a screen without a hardware notch they sit in the pill, with the date as long as the room allows; with a hardware notch the time is in the left ear and the date in the right one, beside the camera. The buttons skip to the previous or next track while music plays, and otherwise open the previous or next tab. Whatever is live, such as music or a working agent, takes the place of the date and the time while it lasts.
