- Displays: a new notch tab that sets the brightness and contrast of each external display, in the display itself as its own buttons would, with a slider for each. Turn it on in Settings, under Notch tabs, Displays.
- Brightness keys for external displays: with the switch on the Displays page, the brightness keys move every external display together and show the level in the look of the volume indicator, with a sun in place of the speaker. While a built-in screen is lit the keys stay with it, and Control with a key sets the external displays. Needs Accessibility.
- System: the heat card now gives the temperature of the processor in degrees, with the state the system reports under it, and a new card shows how fast the fans turn on a Mac that has them.
